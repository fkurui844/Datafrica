About DatAfrica

A Team of Analysts
Built for Africa

DatAfrica is a specialist data analytics team based in Nairobi, Kenya. We bridge the gap between complex data and the decisions that move African organisations forward — bringing together analysts, developers, and visualisation experts under one roof.

From agricultural NGOs to fintech startups and SACCO operators, we've helped clients across East, West, and Southern Africa turn messy spreadsheets and siloed databases into clear, compelling insights that actually get acted on.

  • A multidisciplinary team — analysts, developers & visualisation designers
  • Deep knowledge of African market data structures and challenges
  • End-to-end delivery — from data collection design to final deliverable
  • Fluent in Python, R, SQL, Power BI, Tableau & Excel
  • Experienced in donor-funded, private sector & government projects

What Affects the Price

What Goes Into
Your Quote

Every quote is honest, transparent, and built around your actual needs — not a generic package.

📦
Scope of Work
A quick data clean and summary report costs far less than a full end-to-end BI dashboard with ongoing automation.
🗄️
Data Size & Complexity
How many datasets, how messy the data is, and how many sources need to be merged all affect the effort involved.
📊
Type of Deliverable
A PDF report, an interactive dashboard, a machine learning model, and a custom web system each carry different levels of complexity.
⏱️
Timeline & Urgency
Standard turnarounds are the most affordable. Rush projects can be done — but tight deadlines affect pricing.
🔄
Revisions & Support
Extended support windows, ongoing retainers, or multiple revision rounds are factored in at the proposal stage.
🏗️
Custom System Builds
Full web-based management systems (like MatatuHub or BarberPro) are scoped as separate builds with their own timeline and pricing.
