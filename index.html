About Us

Kenya's Story Told Through Data

Kenya School of Data Analytics is a Nairobi-based data analytics and business intelligence agency. We work with NGOs, startups, corporates, and government bodies across Kenya and beyond — helping them harness the power of their data.

We believe data is only valuable when it drives action. Every dashboard, report, or system we deliver is built with that principle at its core — clear, contextual, and decision-ready.

  • Headquartered in Nairobi, Kenya — serving clients in 12+ countries
  • Expert team across analytics, visualisation, research & engineering
  • Deep understanding of Kenyan business and development contexts
  • End-to-end delivery from raw data to actionable insights
  • Train individuals who are interested in data
  • Interpret organization and business data into clear, usable insight
  • Show people the importance of data within their sectors

What Affects the Price

What Goes Into
Your Quote

Every quote is honest, transparent, and built around your actual needs.

📦
Scope of Work
A quick data summary costs far less than a full BI dashboard with ongoing automation.
🗄️
Data Size & Complexity
How many datasets, how messy the data is, and how many sources need to be merged.
📊
Type of Deliverable
A PDF report, an interactive dashboard, an ML model, and a web system each carry different complexity.
⏱️
Timeline & Urgency
Standard turnarounds are most affordable. Rush projects can be done — but tight deadlines affect pricing.
🔄
Revisions & Support
Extended support windows, ongoing retainers, and multiple revision rounds are factored in upfront.
🏗️
Custom System Builds
Full web-based management systems are scoped as separate builds with their own timeline and pricing.
